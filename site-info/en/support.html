FAQ

  • Which files can I open? DICOM files (.dcm), from the Files app, AirDrop, email or any other app.
  • Do I need internet? No. The DICOM parser runs entirely on your device.
  • Do my images leave my device? No. The app collects nothing, needs no account and uploads nothing.
  • Can I use it for diagnosis? No. It is for viewing and education, not a certified medical device.
