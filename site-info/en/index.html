What does the app do?

  • Full-screen viewer: pinch to zoom, drag to pan, double-tap to reset
  • Cine player — play multi-frame studies as a smooth video loop
  • Window/Level presets for Abdomen, Lung, Bone and Brain
  • Series navigator — slices automatically grouped by series
  • Open .dcm from Files, AirDrop or email — built-in parser, no internet needed
  • Export any frame as PNG or JPEG; dark theme for medical images
